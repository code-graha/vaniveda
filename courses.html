DELF program

Climb the levels

The Diplôme d’Études en Langue Française, awarded by the French Ministry of Education. Valid for life. Pick a rung to see what it covers.

A1 · Discovery

Greetings, introducing yourself, numbers and simple daily interactions.

60 hours · Beginner · Syllabus included

A2 · Survivor

Common expressions about your surroundings, past and future tenses, short professional exchanges.

80 hours · Elementary · Interaction labs

B1 · Threshold

Hold a conversation, handle the unexpected, express opinions and plans, write structured letters.

100 hours · Intermediate · Full grammar

B2 · Vantage

Argue a point of view, build a logical case, speak with fluency and spontaneity — the level most PR applicants target.

120 hours · Upper intermediate · Mock exams

TCF & TEF preparation

Both are recognised by IRCC. The difference is task structure and scoring — we help you pick, then train you to the score.

TCF

TCF Canada

Test de Connaissance du Français. Four mandatory modules — listening, reading, writing, speaking. Results arrive fast, which suits tight application windows.

PR scoring Fast result IRCC recognised

TEF

TEF Canada

Test d’Évaluation de Français. Also fully recognised for Express Entry. A different task format that suits candidates who read quickly and prefer multiple choice.
